People are a mess. Capitalism has shaped us, society has built our habits and before we even know it, our identity has been handed to us served on a silver platter without ever having looked at the menu. Individualism is in our bloods and yet we live as a community, as products for these corporations. 

We have been reduced from people to what we are worth and that is the weakness we are exploited for. Whether it be for data or for money, we let it happen. We **tolerate**. We tolerate the harm done around us, we do not dare look past the surface, we accept things as they are without much question and it is that exact lifestyle that extends to us. To you. Where you begin to **tolerate yourself**. 

Reader I bring your attention to all of this to make you understand the importance of **choice** and how it must be the definitive thing to do in contrast to **tolerance**. Now what does it mean to tolerate myself- you may ask? You do it everyday reader! You are simply unaware of it. You have been tolerating yourself for so long you forget about the act in the first place. It has become so common that it is no longer something you do but rather an engraved part of your identity. 

It is when you settle for less. It is when you let yourself be pushed around. It is when you easily give in to something. It is when you do not ask, just do. It is when you know something is not good for you and yet you still ignore it as if someone else will come and fix it. That stillness in you, that easy acceptance. It is precisely the tolerance I talk about that is wrong with this world and is wrong with **you**! 

You have heard of this from many people, and rightfully so when you talk about the world. But here, I bring you the philosophy of **you**. Who you are as a person is determined by the choices you make to some extent. Slowing down the pace a little, I think it is worth admiring the little agency we get. Be it circumstances or be it fate, not many can exercise agency in their life. So many are ached by lives they did not choose. 

Free will exists, free will does not exists, gurus will tell you both and neither. There will be faithful who dodge the entire question and there will be the common man who could not care less about it. But agency can never be completely removed. You can and always make a choice, significant or irrelevant. Choices that are the opposite of tolerance. 

You see these choices being made, when someone refuses to settle for less. When someone stands their ground. When someone resists. When someone questions actively. When someone does something good for themselves, rightfully knowing its outcome. This antithesis of stillness, it is what choice is. It is resistance to tolerate anything, that defines who you are. 

The act of choosing itself exercises agency that many overlook. So if you take away anything from this, may it be the importance in choice and choosing for yourself instead of tolerance.
